NNA Clicker

WHY I BUILT IT

I farm resources in Clash of Clans across eight accounts. Doing it by hand took too much time and energy, and eight accounts was not realistic to manage manually. NNA Clicker started as the tool I needed to automate those repetitive taps. I still use it every day.

NNA Clicker is an Android touch recorder and auto-clicker. It supports two engines — Accessibility and Shizuku — plus multi-pointer recording, on-device image detection, flow control, and automated release builds.
